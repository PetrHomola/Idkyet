CIRUS Tech - final logo assets

Selected design: segmented chamfered C, open to the right, with two equal-width diagonal cuts.

CIRUS-horizontal: primary company logo.
CIRUS-stacked: compact company lockup.
CIRUS-origin: product lockup.
CIRUS-symbol: standalone emblem.

Each supplied as outlined SVG and transparent PNG, navy #0F3D73, near-black #0B1220, and pure white. White versions are for dark backgrounds. Vector lettering is outlined Nimbus Sans Bold / Regular with adjusted tracking; no fonts required.

Favicon: use dedicated optically adjusted 16px/32px files, with wider cuts. For larger applications use the normal symbol.

Clear space: at least 25% of symbol height. Suggested minimum on-screen widths: CIRUS horizontal 140px, CIRUS Origin 200px. All artwork is a single colour. Do not add gradients, shadows or outlines.

The SVG artwork is the production master, rebuilt from the approved generated concept.
