How we handle data

Business data is all we need for the calculation: what you buy, from whom, at what price and where it comes from. Here you will find exactly what we need, how to send it to us, where it is kept and when we delete it.

01The calculator runs on your side

The calculator works directly in your browser. The list of parts you put into it goes nowhere, and we never see it. You can save the project as a file on your computer. The browser only remembers your language choice.

The only things loaded from the internet are the page itself and its fonts, both from this site. When you upload or export an Excel file, the SheetJS library is also loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. None of these requests carries your data.

The calculator uses no AI. It calculates according to fixed rules described in the methodology.

Offline version

The whole calculator in a single file that works without internet. Useful for computers on an isolated network. Download the offline version

02What we need from you

For a Quick Check or an Evidence Pack, a list of the purchased parts for the product is enough. The easiest way to prepare it is our Excel template.

DataWhy we need it
Part designationTo tell the items apart. It can be your own code, not the real part number.
Description or category (optional)For the breakdown by part category. A generic description is enough, such as “cable harness”.
Supplier and its countryWho to ask for a declaration of origin.
Quantity, price and currencyThe cost of the item. Price excluding transport, duty and VAT.
Country of origin and evidence of originThe calculation itself and an overview of what is documented.

We never take

  • drawings and technical documentation
  • classified information under Czech Act No. 412/2005 Coll.
  • data subject to the US ITAR regulations

If we receive anything like this by mistake, we will not use it, we will delete it and let you know.

03How to send us data

Please do not send the parts list as a plain email attachment. Choose one of these routes:

  • Encrypted archive. ZIP or 7z with AES-256 encryption. Give us the password by phone.
  • Your own storage. You share a folder in your company storage with us, only for named people and for a limited time.
  • Work at your site. The data never leaves your company. We work on your computers (the On-site support service).

05For your IT

Technical details for network and security administrators. You can verify all of it yourself.

  • The offline version is a single HTML file. Once opened, it makes no network requests.
  • The SheetJS 0.18.5 library (reading and writing Excel files) is embedded in the offline version in its official form, with a verified SHA-512 hash.
  • The online calculator sets a Content-Security-Policy in the page head. It allows network connections (connect-src) only to this site. The only external script it may load is SheetJS 0.18.5 from cdnjs, with an integrity check (SRI).
  • The calculator version and the methodology date are shown in the calculator footer.

How to verify the downloaded file

Compute the SHA-256 hash of the downloaded file with:

certutil -hashfile cirus-origin-kalkulacka-offline.html SHA256 Windows shasum -a 256 cirus-origin-kalkulacka-offline.html macOS, Linux

06Contact data and GDPR

Parts lists and purchasing data usually contain no personal data. We mainly process personal data when you contact us.

Controller

What data
Name, company, work email, phone number and the content of the message you send us yourself.
Why
To answer you and to negotiate a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). To stay in touch with you as a business contact (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
How long
For the duration of negotiations and cooperation. If no cooperation follows, we delete the data once we no longer need it, or earlier at your request. Accounting and contract records are kept for as long as the law requires.
Who sees it
We do not sell it or pass it on for marketing. Only the service providers we cannot do without (email and website hosting) have access to it.
Your rights
You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and the right to object. Just write to {{EMAIL}}. You can lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).
