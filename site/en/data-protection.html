How we handle data

For the calculation we only need commercial data: what you buy, from whom, at what price and where it comes from. Here is exactly what we take, what we never take, where the data lives and what happens to it when our work together ends.

01In short

  • The calculator runs in your browser; your data is not sent anywhere. The offline version works without internet.
  • For our services we work only with commercial data: part number or category, supplier, price, country of origin, evidence.
  • We do not take drawings, specifications, technical documentation, classified information or ITAR-controlled data.
  • We keep data in the EU or work directly on your premises. Only summaries by category leave the calculation, exactly as the Commission asks for them.
  • We do not use AI tools on your data without your written consent.
  • We sign your NDA. We are a Czech company with no owners outside the EU.
  • When our work together ends, we delete your data and confirm the deletion.

02The calculator runs on your side

The calculation happens directly in your browser. The bill of materials you paste or upload is not sent anywhere and we never see it. You can save the project as a JSON file on your computer and load it again later. The browser only remembers your language choice.

What the calculator loads from the internet:

  • the page itself and fonts from Google Fonts,
  • the SheetJS library from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, and only when you upload or export an XLSX file.

None of these requests carries your data.

Offline version

The whole calculator in a single HTML file, with SheetJS built in and no fonts from the internet. It also works on a computer with no connection, for example on an isolated network. Download the offline version

03What we take and never take

For a Quick check or an Evidence pack, a “green extract” is enough: a table of purchasing data for the product, following our template. You can replace part numbers with pseudonyms and keep the key yourself.

DataWhat it is for
Part number or categoryTelling lines apart. Can be a pseudonym.
Description (optional)Orientation and category assignment. A generic one is enough, e.g. “cable harness”.
Supplier and its countryWho to ask for a declaration; estimating origin.
Quantity and unit price, currencyLine cost, excluding transport, duty and VAT.
Country of origin and evidenceThe calculation and the evidence status.
Component categoryThe five-category breakdown for the Commission.

We never take

  • drawings,
  • specifications and technical documentation,
  • classified information,
  • ITAR-controlled data.

If we receive anything like this by mistake, we will not use it, we will delete it and let you know.

04Where the data lives

We keep data in the EU. If you do not want it to leave your company, we work directly on your premises, on your devices (the On-site support service).

Only summaries by category leave the calculation, exactly as the Commission asks for them in the “Procurement strategy” template. We do not pass on individual bill of materials lines. In a request for a declaration, a supplier sees only its own parts, never the whole bill of materials.

05AI tools

We do not use AI tools on your data without your written consent. The calculator uses no AI at all; it calculates according to the fixed rules described in the methodology.

06NDA and ownership

We sign your NDA; there is no need to negotiate ours. {{BRAND}} is run by {{COMPANY}}, a Czech company with no owners outside the EU.

07Deletion at the end

When our work together ends, we delete your data and confirm the deletion to you. The deliverables we have handed over stay with you.

08Contact data and GDPR

Bills of materials and purchasing data usually contain no personal data. We mainly process personal data when you contact us.

What data
Name, company, work email, phone number and message content that you send us yourself.
Why
To answer your enquiry and negotiate a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and to keep in touch with you as a business contact (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
How long
For the duration of negotiations and cooperation. If no cooperation follows, we delete the data once we no longer need it, or earlier at your request. Accounting and contract records are kept for the period required by law.
Who sees it
We do not sell it or pass it on for marketing. Only the service providers we strictly need to operate (email, hosting) have access to it.
Your rights
You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and the right to object. Just write to {{EMAIL}}. You can lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).

This website
