What it is about

Components originating outside the EU (for SAFE: outside the EU, EEA EFTA states and Ukraine) may make up no more than 35 % of the component cost of the end product. This applies to SAFE (Regulation 2025/1106, Article 16(10)), EDIP (2025/2643, Article 10), the EDIP Ukraine Support Instrument and the Ukraine loan. If the condition is not met, SAFE payments can be suspended and EDIP grants reduced or terminated; false information can lead to exclusion from EU funding.

We calculate your share using the Commission's method and collect the evidence to back it up: supplier declarations and customs documents.

Who it is for

  • manufacturers and prime contractors on SAFE-funded contracts
  • EDIP applicants and beneficiaries (calls close 16 February 2027)
  • suppliers who have received an origin questionnaire from a prime contractor
  • compliance, purchasing and export control teams

How to start

  1. Download the template ({{WEB}}/sablony/cirus-origin-vstupni-sablona.xlsx) and fill in the purchasing data extract: part number or category, supplier, quantity, price, currency and country of origin if you know it. Part numbers can be pseudonyms.
  2. Send the extract as an encrypted archive (give us the password by phone), or share a folder in your own storage with us. If you wish, we sign your NDA first.
  3. We confirm scope, timing and price. A Quick Check is delivered within 10 working days.
  4. An indicative result right now, for free: the calculator at {{WEB}}/kalkulacka.
