What it is about

Contracts funded by the EU programmes SAFE and EDIP come with a condition: parts made outside the EU may make up at most 35 % of the cost of the product's parts. If you cannot prove it, you risk a suspended payment or a reduced grant.

We find out where your product stands and get the documents from your suppliers to prove it.

Who it is for

  • defence manufacturers who supply SAFE-funded contracts or apply for an EDIP grant (calls close 16 February 2027)
  • parts suppliers whose customer wants a declaration of origin

How to start

  1. Fill in the list of purchased parts for the product: supplier, price and country of origin, if you know it. Excel template: {{WEB}}/sablony/cirus-origin-vstupni-sablona.xlsx
  2. Send it to us as an encrypted archive (ZIP or 7z, AES-256) and give us the password by phone. Do not send it as a plain email attachment. You can also share a folder in your own storage with us.
  3. We confirm the scope, timing and price in writing. If you wish, we sign your NDA first.
