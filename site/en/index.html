SAFE · EDIPOrigin of parts for EU defence funding

We calculate and prove that your product meets the 35 % rule.

Contracts funded by the EU programmes SAFE and EDIP come with a condition: parts made outside the EU may make up at most 35 % of the cost of the product's parts. We find out where you stand and get the documents from your suppliers to prove it.

For whom

Who we help

Defence manufacturers

You supply a product that a government buys with a SAFE loan, or you are applying for an EDIP grant. You have to calculate and document the share of non-EU parts, and keep it documented for the whole contract.

Parts suppliers

Your customer wants a declaration of origin for the parts you supply. We help you determine the origin correctly and prepare the declaration.

Why now

The money is flowing and the condition applies

If you cannot prove it, you risk a suspended payment or a reduced grant. The wording of the rule is in the SAFE Regulation, Article 16(10).

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Did not find your answer? Write to us. The detailed calculation method is in the methodology.

Does this apply to us?

If you supply a product or a part for a contract funded by a SAFE loan or by the EDIP programme, almost certainly yes. The condition applies to the manufacturer of the whole product, so they usually pass it on to their suppliers with an origin questionnaire.

What will you need from us?

A table of the purchased parts for the product: supplier, price and country of origin, if you know it. We need no drawings, technical documentation or classified information. You can replace part numbers with your own designations.

What if a supplier does not respond?

You do not need an answer from everyone. The result shows which suppliers matter, and we contact those first. For imported parts, customs documents help.

Is there an official calculation method for SAFE?

The Commission has not issued one yet, but the rule applies and you have to document it. So we calculate using the method the Commission published for the same rule in the EDIP programme.

The data you need (parts, prices, countries of origin and documents) is the same under any sensible method, so the work is not wasted. When the Commission issues guidance, we recalculate the result from the same data.

Which countries count as the EU?

The EU member states. For SAFE, also Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein and Ukraine. Switzerland and the United Kingdom do not. The rules for each programme are in the methodology.

Is this legal advice?

No. We deliver the calculation and the documents from suppliers. The declarations of origin are signed by the suppliers, and your company is responsible for the information it submits to the contracting authority. Disputed cases are resolved with a customs specialist.

How long does it take?

The calculator gives you a result straight away. A Quick Check takes up to 10 working days, an Evidence Pack 4–6 weeks. Supplier replies take the longest, so we first contact the suppliers the result depends on.
